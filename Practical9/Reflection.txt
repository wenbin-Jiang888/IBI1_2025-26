This practical was about building stochastic SIR models to simulate infectious disease spread.
I learned how to translate real epidemic problems into probabilistic models using Python.
The basic SIR model was straightforward, while the spatial 2D model was more challenging, especially handling neighbor infections and grid updates.
Vaccination simulation helped me understand herd immunity intuitively.
The most important thing I learned is how randomness and population structure affect disease dynamics.
Overall, it was interesting and useful to see how models can predict epidemic trends.
